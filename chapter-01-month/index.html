Happy Monthsary, My Love

Happy monthsary, my love.

I want to take a moment today to remind you how much you truly mean to me. Life can get busy and days go by so fast, but I always want to make sure you know just how special you are. Every single day with you is a gift, and reaching another month together makes me realize just how lucky I am to have you by my side.

Looking back at our memories, one day that always stays in my heart is your birthday on August 28. We went out with our friends, both my classmates and yours, starting from Starbuks in Cebu, planning to hike all the way through Spartan Trail. Even though the rain poured and we didn't get to finish the Spartan route, that day became so unforgettable to me.

After we arrived at Starbuks, we ate dinner. Then I asked you, “Magpa-pic ta adto dapit sa signage sa unahan?” Kay sige man ka ug ingon nga mag-picture ta didto, so niadto ta.

Funny kaayo kay naglibog pa ko unsaon nako pagsulti sa imoha, gyud—HAHAHAHA. Funny kaayo ta pag panguyab, mura ra tag wala, noh? Char. But I am truly grateful for that day, for real. The view was romantic, and simple ra kaayo akong pag panguyab nimo, babe. I hope you appreciated it gyud. After that, we set out for Spartan.

We were all so exhausted, drenched in sweat, and tired from the climb, but the entire time, having you beside me gave me all the strength I needed. Seeing you there kept me going, and all I wanted was to look out for you, take care of you, and make sure you were safe and okay every step of the trail. The muddy paths, the heavy rain, and the tired legs didn't matter at all because I was sharing it with you. That hike showed me that as long as we are together, even when plans change and the trail gets tough, we can get through anything with a smile.

Thank you for being my partner, my peace, and my favorite person. You make every simple moment feel like home, and every hard day so much easier just by being there.

I know no journey is completely smooth, but my choice will always be you. I promise to keep holding your hand, caring for you, and walking beside you no matter what weather or trail comes our way.

Happy monthsary again, my love. I love you so much, more than words can say, always and forever.

Always yours,
Ivan
